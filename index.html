How I work

From pain to product
01

Find the real pain

I start with people, not pixels — interviews, workflows and data, until the real problem is clear.

02

Draft to prototype

I plan the solution from rough drafts to a working prototype, and put it in front of users and the team early.

03

Iterate & hand over

Feedback drives the next round. I refine, hand over to the team, and loop back when new feedback lands.
