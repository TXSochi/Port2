(01)

Overview

Hydra Supply is an MVP for an ERP-based supply chain and order tracking solution between a supplier, Hydra Supply, and its retail buyers such as Minimart+. The primary focus: let buyers log in and follow every shipment in detail — more transparency and less back-and-forth for both sides.

(02)

The problem

Procurement staff faced significant operational friction due to disjointed order tracking and rigid supply chain software. Traditional ordering processes were plagued by lack of real-time status visibility, complex catalog navigation and tedious manual approval steps — leading to delays in stock replenishment and miscommunication with suppliers.

01 · Problem

Users struggle with

  • Lack of real-time tracking for shipment details
  • Difficulty accessing complete order information
  • Inefficient communication about delivery status
02 · Why

Why it’s needed

  • Clients still phone in to track their orders
  • No platform gives real-time shipment data
  • Without it, businesses risk losing clients to competitors with better tracking
03 · Goal

Objectives

  • An MVP focused on the essential: log in and track detailed shipment information
  • Clear, real-time updates on shipment status and order details
  • A smoother supply chain for both suppliers and buyers
(03)

(05)

Competitor analysis

Shipper, Maersk and the Hydra Supply MVP, compared on what matters to SME and retail buyers: real-time tracking, clarity and support.

PlatformUnique valueAdvantagesDrawbacks

Maersk

Unique value

Fast delivery and an intuitive dashboard keep users’ trust, but lack deeper insight into product-level shipment status.

Advantages

Clean interface, good mobile accessibility, a broad same-day network and strong marketplace integrations.

Drawbacks

Tracking lacks a detailed shipment breakdown; the dashboard is geared to volume shippers rather than SME partners.

Shipper

Unique value

Shows why operational transparency and status documentation matter — and why smaller partners need it simpler.

Advantages

Enterprise-grade tracking with clear shipment milestones, document management and customizable workflows.

Drawbacks

Complex UI built for large corporate logistics, with a steep learning curve for SME users.

Hydra SupplyOur MVP

Unique value

Intentionally lightweight to validate core assumptions first — ideal for iterative development and testing.

Advantages

Focuses on the essentials: real-time shipment visibility, ETA and simple order tracking, built mobile-first for buyers on the go.

Drawbacks

No integrations, historical insights or invoicing workflows yet — scoped on purpose to the most urgent pain points.

Hydra Supply differentiates by simplifying — not overloading — the user journey. Shipper and Maersk excel at advanced logistics; this MVP focuses on real-time visibility, clarity and speed for smaller retail buyers.

User flow

How an order travels between the buyer, the Hydra app, the supplier and the courier — and where the buyer steps in to check, verify or raise an issue.

  1. 01 · Buyer

    Place order

    A recurring bulk purchase order.

  2. 02 · Supplier

    Pack & dispatch

    Hydra Supply prepares the order and hands it over.

  3. 03 · Courier

    Pick up

    A courier is assigned and collects the parcel.

  4. 04 · Hydra app

    In shipping

    The status changes and the buyer gets a push notification.

  5. 05 · Buyer

    Log in

    Email and password, with remember-me.

  6. 06 · Hydra app

    Dashboard

    Active deliveries with ETA and item counts, filterable by status.

  7. 07 · Hydra app

    Order detail

    Progress, ETA, courier contact, items and a live map from the courier.

  8. 08 · Hydra app

    All items delivered?

    Ordered vs delivered, item by item.

    Yes → verifyNo → stock issue
  9. 09 · Buyer

    Verify

    Confirm the delivery and download the invoice; the order moves to history.

  10. 10 · Supplier

    Stock issue → support

    Sold-out or partial items are flagged; the buyer can call the courier or report an issue.
