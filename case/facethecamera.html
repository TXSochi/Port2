Work/03

AI Teleprompter

A modern web application and marketing experience for an AI-powered teleprompter tool, enabling business professionals and creators to record confident, line-free video presentations directly from their web browsers.

Role
Website Designer
Category
AI · Web App · Web Design
Platform
Desktop · Mobile
Timeline
3 months
(01)

The problem

Many professionals and content creators struggle when recording video pitches, product demos and presentations. Memorizing long scripts is time-consuming, while traditional teleprompters often lead to awkward, off-camera eye movement, clutter and unpolished delivery.

(02)

Who it’s for

Business executives & founders

Recording company updates, pitch decks and investor presentations.

Sales & marketing teams

Delivering personalized product demos, client outreach videos and sales follow-ups.

Content creators & educators

Producing structured online courses, social media videos and tutorials efficiently.

(03)

What I designed

Product landing page

A sleek, modern dark-mode website designed to clearly articulate AI-driven features, build product credibility and drive user adoption.

Smart teleprompter studio

A minimal, distraction-free web app interface featuring live camera preview and script overlay controls.

Eye-level script positioning UI

Adaptive layout logic that anchors text scrolling close to the camera lens to maintain steady, natural eye contact.

Pace & highlight controls

Intuitive settings for custom scrolling speed, font scaling and keypoint highlights for effortless speech rhythm.

AI on every takeEye contact correctionFluentVoiceSyncFixExpression relaxation
(04)

How I approached it

01 — Market & competitor analysis

Analyzed existing video recording platforms to identify UI clutter and friction points during live camera sessions.

02 — User flow & wireframing

Structured seamless web onboarding and recording flows, prioritizing rapid script editing and immediate previewing.

03 — High-fidelity web design

Developed a modern, dark-themed visual layout aligned with contemporary AI product aesthetics.

04 — Responsive layouts & handoff

Designed fully responsive layouts for desktop web and mobile browsers, preparing structured assets for developer implementation.
